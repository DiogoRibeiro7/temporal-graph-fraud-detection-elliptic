# Model Risk and Governance

A fraud-risk score is not evidence.

## Main risks

- false positives can burden legitimate users
- graph proximity can produce guilt-by-association errors
- historical labels may be incomplete or biased
- fraud strategies may change over time
- unlabelled nodes make performance estimates uncertain

## Controls required before real use

- independent validation
- temporal backtesting
- human review
- audit logs
- drift monitoring
- model-risk review
- clear appeal and correction processes
